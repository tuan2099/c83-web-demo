## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the structural integrity, industrial stature, and forward-looking scale of a premier Vietnamese construction and materials conglomerate. The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** discipline with high-impact industrial architectural cues: heavy typographics, engineered micro-details, technical framing, and disciplined geometric rhythm.

The UI must evoke uncompromised reliability, national infrastructure authority, and commercial vitality. Key principles include:
- **Architectural Rigor:** Layouts prioritize clarity, structural grids, and tangible substance. Every module feels built, not merely rendered.
- **Engineered Precision:** Technical eyebrow labels, calibrated borders, and purposeful contrast establish high trust for B2B stakeholders, project developers, and institutional partners.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Deep navy structural surfaces anchor the viewport, while purposeful warm gold accents denote premium action points, bids, and strategic inquiries.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around engineered balance: high-visibility structural blues, authoritative deep navy grounding, energetic gold calls-to-action, and clinical architectural neutrals.

### Roles & Tokens
- **Primary Blue (`#1D75BC`):** Brand baseline for functional controls, hyperlinks, active pagination nodes, active status badges, and technical accents.
- **Deep Navy (`#2B3990`):** Secondary authority color. Governs deep industrial footers, executive banners, dark-mode cards, and forms the finishing terminus of the standard diagonal hero gradient.
- **Accent Gold Gradient (`#FDB913` → `#F39C12`):** Reserved for high-conversion CTAs ("Request Quote", "Procurement Inquiry"). Paired strictly with `#1B2440` typography and a calibrated gold glow shadow (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(253, 185, 19, 0.45)`).
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - **Foreground Core (`#1B2440`):** Deep navy-charcoal for dense editorial contrast across titles and high-priority data points.
  - **Muted Text (`#6B7385`):** Standard neutral for descriptive copy, metadata, specifications, and secondary metrics.
- **Surfaces & Borders:**
  - **Canvas Base:** `#FFFFFF`
  - **Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF`
  - **Muted Surface:** `#F2F5F9` (zebra striping, table headers, inner panel wells)
  - **Alternate Surface:** `#EEF3F8` (metric readouts, technical data-sheet containers)
  - **Structural Border:** `#E3E9F0` (consistent 1px framing on all surface boundaries)

## Typography

The typography system relies solely on **Be Vietnam Pro** to ensure native typographic accuracy, pristine diacritic rendering for Vietnamese business communication, and contemporary geometric presence.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Display & Headings:** Set with an ultra-dense weight (`900` Black) and compact line heights to create the visual equivalent of reinforced concrete beams—solid, commanding, and monolithic.
- **Eyebrow Pre-Headings (`label-eyebrow`):** Rendered strictly uppercase with `0.25em` tracking in Primary Blue (`#1D75BC`). Eyebrows must always be accompanied by horizontal decorative anchor rules (`h: 1px`, `w: 24px`, background: `#1D75BC`) either inline or flanking the text.
- **Body Text:** Standardized on `400` (Regular) and `500` (Medium) weights to guarantee immediate legibility across technical tender specifications, product certifications, and material safety documents.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored to a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display heavy data sets, catalog indices, and multi-tier logistics dashboards.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, max container width of `1280px`, `gutter` of `1.5rem` (`24px`), `margin` of `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `gutter` of `1.5rem` (`24px`), `margin` of `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter` of `1rem` (`16px`), `margin` of `1rem` (`16px`).

### Spatial Principles
- Inner component paddings follow an 8pt architectural rhythm: `space-xs` (4px), `space-sm` (8px), `space-md` (16px), `space-lg` (24px), and `space-xl` (40px).
- Vertical stack sequences maintain consistent section clearances of `80px` on desktop and `48px` on mobile screens to separate distinct operational divisions and product series.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids generic gray drop shadows. Instead, it relies on structural border definition combined with a signature **diffused industrial blue ambient shadow**.

### Elevation Scale
- **Flat Surface (`elevation-0`):** Default card and canvas state. Framed with a 1px solid border in `#E3E9F0`.
- **Card Rest State (`elevation-1`):** Border `1px solid #E3E9F0`, overlaid with subtle diffused blue elevation:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 40px -12px rgba(29, 117, 188, 0.18);`
- **Interactive Card Hover State (`elevation-2`):** Elevates via a micro-lift transform (`translateY(-4px)`), increasing blue density:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -12px rgba(29, 117, 188, 0.30); border-color: rgba(29, 117, 188, 0.35);`
- **Gold Accent Glow (`elevation-accent`):** Exclusively paired with the Gold primary CTA button:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(253, 185, 19, 0.45);`
- **Inner-Page Hero Depth:** Built using a continuous `135deg` linear gradient from `#1D75BC` to `#2B3990`. Visual depth is introduced via a subtle foreground CSS dot pattern (1px dots on 20px centers, opacity `0.08`) and a radial blurred ambient gold light source (`w: 320px`, `h: 320px`, `blur: 120px`, background: `#FDB913`, opacity `0.15`) placed at the lower-right visual axis.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances technical discipline with ergonomic touch targets. 

- **Structural Elements (Inputs, Panels, Cards, Dialogs):** Fixed at an exact corner radius of **8px** (`0.5rem`). This preserves an engineered, monolithic silhouette without appearing sharp or brutalist.
- **Categorical & Navigation Elements (Badges, Filter Chips, Status Pills):** Standardized on full **pill radius** (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish contextual interactive filters and operational statuses from content cards.
- **Nested Radii Rule:** Child elements nested inside 8px cards maintain a 4px corner radius if inset by 8px or more, preserving harmonious concentric geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion CTA (Gold Gradient):**
  - **Background:** Linear gradient `135deg, #FDB913 0%, #F39C12 100%`.
  - **Typography:** `label-button`, text color `#1B2440` (Dark Navy).
  - **Border:** None. Radius: `8px`. Padding: `12px 24px`.
  - **Elevation:** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(253, 185, 19, 0.45)`.
  - **Hover:** Brightness filter `1.05`, translateY `-1px`.
- **Secondary Action (Corporate Blue):**
  - **Background:** Solid `#1D75BC`. Text: `#FFFFFF`. Radius: `8px`.
  - **Hover:** Background `#16629F`.
- **Outline / Tertiary Button:**
  - **Background:** Transparent. Border: `1.5px solid #1D75BC`. Text: `#1D75BC`. Radius: `8px`.
  - **Hover:** Background `#F2F5F9`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Shape:** Pill (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Unselected:** Background `#F2F5F9`, border `1px solid #E3E9F0`, text `#6B7385`, font size `13px`, font weight `600`, padding `6px 16px`.
- **Selected / Active:** Background `#1D75BC`, border `1px solid #1D75BC`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards & Industrial Containers
- **Construction:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E3E9F0`, radius `8px`, padding `24px`.
- **Shadow:** `0 12px 40px -12px rgba(29, 117, 188, 0.18)`.
- **Hover Behavior:** `translateY(-4px)`, shadow shifts to `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(29, 117, 188, 0.30)`, border color transitions to `rgba(29, 117, 188, 0.35)` across `200ms ease`.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container:** Background `#FFFFFF`, height `48px`, border `1px solid #E3E9F0`, radius `8px`, padding `0 16px`.
- **Text:** `#1B2440`, placeholder `#6B7385`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#1D75BC`, outline: `2px solid rgba(29, 117, 188, 0.20)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Unchecked:** Border `1.5px solid #E3E9F0`, background `#FFFFFF`. Radius `4px` (checkbox), `9999px` (radio).
- **Checked:** Background `#1D75BC`, border color `#1D75BC`, white checkmark or center pip.

### Pagination
- **Nodes:** Radius `8px`, size `40px x 40px`, border `1px solid #E3E9F0`, text `#1B2440`.
- **Active Node:** Background `#1D75BC`, border color `#1D75BC`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight `700`.

### Technical Eyebrow Block
- **Layout:** Flex row, items centered, gap `12px`.
- **Decorators:** Left and/or right accent bar (`width: 24px`, `height: 2px`, background `#1D75BC`).
- **Text:** `label-eyebrow`, color `#1D75BC`, transform uppercase.
